## Two different approaches

_This is placeholder content — replace it with the real article from the CMS._

Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) focuses on identifying and changing unhelpful thought patterns in the present. Psychodynamic therapy looks further back, exploring how past experiences shape current behavior.

## Which one fits you

Neither approach is universally "better" — the right fit depends on what you're working through, and how you like to process things.
