## It's not always obvious

Anxiety doesn't always show up as panic. Sometimes it looks like procrastination, irritability, or trouble sleeping — signs that are easy to write off as "just stress."

## The five signs

- Constant low-level worry that doesn't resolve
- Trouble concentrating or making decisions
- Physical tension — jaw clenching, shallow breathing
- Avoiding situations that didn't used to bother you
- Sleep that doesn't leave you rested
